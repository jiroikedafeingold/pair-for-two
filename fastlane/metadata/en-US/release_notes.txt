• Scoreboard mode — playing with real cards? Lay the phone or iPad between you and use it as the board. A slider each, the right way up for both of you, and the score in the middle turns to face whoever just pegged.
• Achievements and a leaderboard — first win, skunks, a 24-point hand, the perfect 29, a comeback from 30 behind, five in a row, and lifetime wins, all through Game Center.
• Your record — every game is kept: wins, streaks, skunks, best hand and time played, with a history you can look back through.
• Steadier connections — pairing two devices is more patient and more reliable, especially with no Wi-Fi network around, and a game now rebuilds itself after a drop, a long spell in your pocket, or even a force quit.
• Whose card is that? — when the crib is counted, a small marker in each player's color sits under the cards they put in.
• Last card announced — when the final card of a hand goes down, both devices say so, so nobody misses the point.
• Kinder to larger text — with bigger text sizes turned on, the counting prompt and its buttons no longer get cut off, and long buttons wrap instead of running off the edge.
• Room to breathe on iPad — bigger buttons and a bigger board, sized for a device sitting between two players.
• You keep score by default — new games start with the counting up to the players, and the welcome tour shows where to hand it to the app instead (Settings, then Scoring).
