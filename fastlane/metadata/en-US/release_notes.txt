• Steadier connections — pairing two phones is more patient and more reliable, especially when there's no Wi-Fi network around, and a game now recovers on its own after one phone has been closed or set aside for a while.
• Whose card is that? — when the crib is counted, a small marker in each player's colour sits under the cards they put in.
• Last card announced — when the final card of a hand goes down, both phones say so, so nobody misses the point.
• Kinder to larger text — with bigger text sizes turned on, the counting prompt and its buttons no longer get cut off, and long buttons wrap instead of running off the edge.
• Tidier counting screen — the prompt and the Continue button now line up with the cards being counted, and the scoring chips have room of their own instead of crowding them.
• You keep score by default — new games start with scoring up to the players, and the welcome tour now shows where to hand the counting to the app instead (Settings, then Scoring).
